Interests

My research interests focus on the application of geospatial technologies to environmental monitoring, territorial analysis, and sustainability assessment. I am particularly interested in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, satellite image processing, spatial modeling, and multitemporal analysis of land-use and land-cover changes.

I am also interested in Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) technologies, aerial photogrammetry, high-resolution geospatial mapping, and topographic and geodetic surveying using Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS). My research explores the integration of satellite imagery, UAV data, and geospatial analysis to support environmental assessment, territorial planning, and sustainable development.
